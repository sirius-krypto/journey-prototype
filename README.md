# Integrated Journey Prototype

A clickable concept prototype of an integrated bank and insurance journey in Singapore. A fictional bank, **Global Bank**, works with three insurer partners: **a life insurer**, **an international health insurer** and **a general insurer** (lifestyle protection: motor, travel and home). One synthetic family, every journey on the map, on the devices people would really use: the client's iPhone, the relationship manager's desktop and the insurance specialist's iPad.

**A concept, not a product.** The family and the staff are synthetic, every number is illustrative and there is no real client data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any bank or insurer: "Global Bank" and the insurer names are generic placeholders. Singapore's public schemes (SGFinDex, Singpass, MediSave, Integrated Shield plans, MAS rules) are named as they are publicly described.

## Open it

- Online: https://sirius-krypto.github.io/journey-prototype/
- Offline: download `index.html` and double-click it. It makes no network calls.

Best on a laptop at 1280×800 or larger. On the welcome screen, choose **Play the story, guided**.
